## Website presentation recommendation

### Suggested heading
**Manufacturing & Quality Standards**

### Suggested introductory copy
> Aaliis Pharmaceuticals works with qualified pharmaceutical manufacturing partners selected with attention to GMP compliance, quality-management systems, regulatory documentation, and manufacturing capability. The information below reflects the supporting documentation and public manufacturer information available to us.

### Suggested manufacturer cards
Each card should show only:

1. Manufacturer name
2. Manufacturing licence
3. GMP / WHO-GMP status
4. ISO certifications
5. Regulatory information
6. Manufacturing capabilities
7. Quality / reliability assessment

Do **not** include manufacturer phone numbers, emails, sales links, or promotional calls-to-action.

### Important wording rule
Use phrases such as **“stated by the manufacturer,” “documented in supplier records,” or “verified from available documentation”** where appropriate. Do not claim that every facility is independently audited by Aaliis unless that is actually true.
